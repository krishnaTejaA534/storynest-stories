# The Brave Little Lion

## Page 1

In the middle of a bright green forest lived a little lion named Leo.

Leo had a big heart, but he was afraid of many things.

He was afraid of dark caves, loud thunder, and even tiny spiders.

---

## Page 2

One morning, Leo's best friend, a little rabbit named Pip, came running through the forest.

"Leo! Leo! I need your help!"

Leo quickly stood up.

"What happened?"

---

## Page 3

Pip pointed toward the river.

"My little sister is stuck on the other side!"

The river was flowing faster than usual.

Leo looked at the water.

He felt scared.

---

## Page 4

"I don't think I can do it," Leo whispered.

Pip looked at him.

"You don't have to be fearless to be brave."

Leo thought about those words.

Maybe being brave meant helping someone even when you were scared.

---

## Page 5

Leo walked toward the river.

His paws trembled.

He took one deep breath.

Then another.

Slowly, he stepped onto the old wooden bridge.

---

## Page 6

Halfway across, a loud crack came from the bridge.

Leo froze.

The wind blew around him.

But then he heard Pip's little sister calling.

"Leo!"

Leo remembered why he had come.

---

## Page 7

He carefully moved forward.

One paw.

Then another.

Finally, he reached the other side.

Pip's little sister jumped happily into his arms.

"You came!"

---

## Page 8

Leo carried her safely back across the bridge.

When they reached the forest, all the animals cheered.

"You are so brave!" they shouted.

Leo smiled.

---

## Page 9

"I was actually very scared," Leo admitted.

Pip laughed.

"That's what makes it brave!"

Leo realized that courage wasn't about never being afraid.

It was about doing the right thing even when fear was there.

---

## Page 10

That evening, Leo sat beneath his favorite tree.

The forest was quiet.

The stars began to appear above him.

Leo still felt afraid of some things.

But now he knew something important.

Whenever someone needed him, he could find the courage to help.

The End.
