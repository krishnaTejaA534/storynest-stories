# Princess of the Stars

## Page 1

Far beyond the clouds, in a castle made of silver light, lived a young princess named Luna.

Luna was different from other princesses.

She loved exploring the night sky.

---

## Page 2

Every evening, Luna climbed the tallest tower of the castle.

From there, she could see thousands of stars sparkling above the kingdom.

Her favorite star was a tiny golden one.

She called it Twinkle.

---

## Page 3

One night, Luna noticed that Twinkle was not shining.

The little star looked tired and dim.

"What's wrong?" Luna asked.

"I have forgotten how to shine," Twinkle whispered.

---

## Page 4

Luna wanted to help.

She carried Twinkle to the Moon Garden.

There, flowers glowed softly beneath the silver moonlight.

"Perhaps the garden can help you remember," Luna said.

---

## Page 5

They walked past glowing roses and sleepy blue flowers.

But Twinkle remained dim.

Then Luna heard a tiny sound.

Someone was crying.

---

## Page 6

Behind a tall flower sat a little cloud creature.

"I lost my way home," it said.

Luna smiled.

"Don't worry. We'll help you."

Twinkle suddenly began to glow a little brighter.

---

## Page 7

Luna and Twinkle guided the little cloud through the sky.

Soon, they found its family waiting among the clouds.

The little creature hugged Luna.

"Thank you!"

---

## Page 8

Twinkle began shining brighter than ever.

"I understand now," the star said.

"Stars shine brightest when they help light the way for others."

Luna smiled.

---

## Page 9

Together, Luna and Twinkle returned to the castle.

As they arrived, every star in the sky began to sparkle.

The whole kingdom looked like a magical blanket of diamonds.

---

## Page 10

Luna looked up at the sky.

She knew that even the smallest light could make a big difference.

Twinkle winked beside her.

"Goodnight, Princess Luna."

"Goodnight, little star."

And the peaceful kingdom fell asleep beneath the sparkling sky.

The End.
