# The Little Moon Rabbit

## Page 1

High above the sleeping world, where the stars twinkled like tiny lanterns, lived a little rabbit named Milo.

Milo lived on the Moon with his grandmother. Every night, he loved to hop across the silver hills and watch the stars.

---

## Page 2

One evening, Milo noticed something unusual.

One tiny star was missing from the sky.

"Oh no!" Milo whispered. "Where could it have gone?"

He looked behind the clouds, but the little star was nowhere to be seen.

---

## Page 3

Milo decided to search for his missing friend.

He packed three carrot biscuits into a tiny blue bag and set off across the Moon.

The silver dust sparkled beneath his little paws.

---

## Page 4

Milo hopped over a tall moon hill.

Then he jumped across a glowing crater.

Suddenly, he heard a tiny voice.

"Help me!"

Milo stopped and listened carefully.

---

## Page 5

Behind a fluffy silver cloud, Milo found the missing star.

It was tangled in a ribbon of moonlight.

"Don't worry," Milo said. "I'll help you."

He carefully pulled the ribbon away.

---

## Page 6

The little star began to glow.

"Thank you, Milo!" it said.

Milo smiled.

"Every little light deserves a chance to shine."

Together, they started their journey back home.

---

## Page 7

As they travelled, they passed sleepy clouds and dancing comets.

The little star told Milo about all the places it had seen from the sky.

Milo listened happily.

---

## Page 8

At last, they reached the highest hill on the Moon.

The little star floated gently into the sky.

Suddenly, the whole night became brighter.

All the other stars twinkled with joy.

---

## Page 9

Milo waved goodbye.

"Goodnight, little star!"

"Goodnight, Milo!" the star replied.

Milo hopped back toward his cozy home.

---

## Page 10

When Milo reached home, Grandma Rabbit was waiting.

"You were very brave tonight," she said.

Milo climbed into his warm little bed.

Through his window, the little star gave him one final twinkle.

Milo smiled and closed his eyes.

And beneath the peaceful moonlight, he drifted into a happy sleep.

The End.
